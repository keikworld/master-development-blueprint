# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior

- **Be welcoming** — Every contributor was a beginner once. Write for the person who will read this at 2 AM, frustrated, trying to figure out why their code doesn't work.
- **Be respectful** — Disagreement is fine. Personal attacks are not. Critique ideas, not people.
- **Be constructive** — If you see a problem, suggest a fix. "This is wrong" is less useful than "This is wrong because X, and here's how to fix it."
- **Assume good intent** — Most contributions come from a desire to help. Ask clarifying questions before assuming the worst.

### Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination of any kind
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sexual language or imagery in any project space
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Ethical Use Policy

**This blueprint is intended to help people build legitimate software — secure, compliant, well-engineered applications that solve real problems.**

### Permitted Use
- Building commercial or open-source software projects
- Learning software engineering practices
- Teaching others how to build secure applications
- Creating tools that improve people's lives
- Research and education

### Prohibited Use
- **Building malware, ransomware, or malicious software** — Do not use these patterns to create software designed to harm individuals, organizations, or systems
- **Building surveillance or tracking tools** without explicit informed consent of the people being tracked
- **Developing software that discriminates** against protected groups
- **Creating tools for unauthorized access** to systems or data (pentesting is only permitted with explicit authorization)
- **Any use that violates applicable laws** in your jurisdiction

### Good-Faith Contribution

If you believe a contribution or use of this project violates these guidelines, please report it by opening an issue or contacting the maintainers. We take ethical concerns seriously.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement at the project's GitHub issues. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
